The labs

Where EE coursework gets built, measured and debugged.

SEC 1.110 and 1.111

EE Active Learning Labs

These are the teaching labs for electrical engineering courses. Students work through course labs and projects here, with staff and teaching fellows nearby.

Access is organized in tiers. See Access and training for how to get in.

SEC 1.108

EE Shop

The EE Shop is an electronics shop that supports project and research work. It sits next to the labs and is part of the same team.

Questions about the shop can go through Support.
