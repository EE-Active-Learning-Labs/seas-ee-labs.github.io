Lab safety

These rules apply to everyone, every visit.

Every visit

  • Wear the right protectionClosed-toe shoes and long pants. Safety glasses, gloves and a lab coat whenever your task calls for them.
  • Know the emergency gearFind the eyewash, safety shower, first aid kit and exits before you start.
  • Keep food and drink outNothing to eat or drink at the benches.
  • Stay put when equipment is runningDo not leave lasers, heat sources or reactions unattended.
  • Ask before you try something newIf a step is unfamiliar, ask lab or teaching staff first.

When something goes wrong

  • Stop and make it safeTurn equipment off if you can do so safely, and move away from the hazard.
  • Get helpTell the nearest staff member right away. In an emergency, call Harvard University Police.
  • Report it afterwardSpills, breakage and near misses all get reported, even small ones.
