Equipment

A general list of the instruments and fabrication tools in the EE Active Learning Labs and the EE Shop.

Most equipment stays at its bench. Items marked for checkout can be borrowed from the pool. Use of any equipment follows the safety rules and the permitted project work limits. Model names link to manuals or datasheets where available.
