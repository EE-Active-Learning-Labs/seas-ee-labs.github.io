Lab safety

These rules apply to everyone, every visit. Access to the labs is tiered by training, supervision and the work you plan to do.

Every visit

  • Wear the right protectionClosed-toe shoes and long pants. Safety glasses, gloves and a lab coat whenever your task calls for them.
  • Know the emergency gearFind the eyewash, safety shower, first aid kit and exits before you start.
  • Keep food and drink outNothing to eat or drink at the benches.
  • Stay put when equipment is runningDo not leave lasers, heat sources, chargers or reactions unattended.
  • Stay inside the project boundsAll work must follow the permitted project work limits on voltage, current, batteries and other hazards.
  • Ask before you try something newIf a step is unfamiliar, ask lab or teaching staff first.

When something goes wrong

  • Stop and make it safeTurn equipment off if you can do so safely, and move away from the hazard.
  • Get helpTell the nearest staff member right away. In an emergency, call Harvard University Police.
  • Damaged or swelling batteryMove it to the designated sand bucket and notify lab staff. Never put a damaged battery in the trash.
  • Report it afterwardSpills, breakage and near misses all get reported, even small ones.

Tiered access

Who can use the labs, when, and with what supervision depends on training completed, prerequisite courses, the nature of the work, academic need and whether the work has been safety-vetted. This applies to all undergraduate and graduate students using the labs for coursework, independent projects or research, during or outside scheduled class sessions.

Staffed and supervised

Tier 1

  • Hours9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday, during daytime lab hours.
  • WhoAny student enrolled in a course with a planned lab activity in the EE Active Learning Labs.
  • SupervisionLab staff or a designated lab monitor or proctor must be present.
  • Project workStudents who have not completed ES 50 or ES 152 and want to do course-related project work must submit a project plan for review by lab staff.
  • UseThe default level for students who have not met Tier 2 criteria, or whose work has not been vetted for unsupervised use.
Unsupervised

Tier 2

  • Hours9:00 AM to 9:30 PM, seven days a week.
  • PrerequisiteCompletion of ES 50 or ES 152, plus one of two pathways.
  • Coursework pathwayThe specific lab or experiment has been reviewed and vetted by lab staff as safe to perform unsupervised.
  • Project pathwayYour project plan has been reviewed and approved by lab staff. The plan's timeline must end within the current academic year (May).
  • SupervisionNone required on site, but safety protocols must be in place.

During unsupervised access, students take full responsibility for their work and conduct in the lab. Lab staff cannot be held responsible for outcomes of unsupervised activity performed under approved procedures. Tier 2 review is about safety, and it also gives you time to think through safety, feasibility, timeline and resources before you begin.

Getting Tier 2 approval for a project

For students seeking Tier 2 access for independent project work.

  1. Confirm the prerequisiteShow that you have completed ES 50 or ES 152.
  2. Submit a project planDescribe the scope, equipment, procedures, expected activities and a timeline that ends within the current academic year (May).
  3. Staff reviewLab staff review the plan for hazard level and feasibility given your training and experience.
  4. DecisionThe plan is approved, approved with conditions (for example, limited equipment or checkpoint check-ins), or denied.
  5. Added to the rosterApproved students join the Tier 2 roster for the project, ending no later than May of the current academic year.
