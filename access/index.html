Access and training

Everyone who works in the labs completes training first. Here is the path.

The path

  1. TrainComplete the required EHS training and the lab safety orientation.
  2. RequestSubmit an access request for the space and tier you need.
  3. ConfirmYou get an email when your access is set up.

Request lab access

Access tiers

Your tier decides whether you work with supervision.

Tier 1

Supervised access

You work while lab staff or teaching staff are present. This is where most students start, and it covers course lab sessions.

Tier 2

Independent access

You work on your own in the lab. This tier requires completing ES50 or ES152 first, plus the training above.

Questions

Do I need access for my course lab session?

Scheduled course lab sessions run with your teaching staff, so you work under supervised access. Complete the safety orientation before your first session.

How do I move up to independent access?

Finish the prerequisite course, keep your training current, and submit a new access request that names the tier you want.

Who do I ask if my request is stuck?

Use the support page and mention the date you applied.
