Access

Lab access is tied to the courses you are enrolled in and to the specific needs of those courses.

Lab access is for students enrolled in SEAS courses that have planned activities supported through the labs.

Tied to your course

Enrollment comes first

We open the labs to students whose courses have planned lab activities. Your enrollment is what connects you to a space and a schedule.

Tied to course needs

Every course is different

Each course has its own equipment, safety needs and procedures, so what you do first depends on the course you are taking.

Where to look

Your course page

Most courses post their access procedures on the course Canvas site. Your course page here points you there.

Find your course

Questions

Where are the access steps for my course?

Open your course from the course list. Most courses post access procedures on their Canvas site, and your course page says where to look.

My course is not on the list

Ask your course staff first. If you still need help, use the support page.

Who do I ask if I am stuck?

Use the support page and name your course.
