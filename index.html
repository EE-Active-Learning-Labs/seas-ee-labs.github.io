How access works

Access is for students enrolled in SEAS courses that have planned activities supported through the labs. If that is you, three steps stand between you and the bench.

  1. Find your coursePick it from the course list.
  2. Follow its stepsMost courses post access procedures on their Canvas site.
  3. WorkCome to your planned lab activities, and book tech support when you need it.
